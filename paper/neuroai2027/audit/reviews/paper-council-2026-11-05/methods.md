Findings

M1. Major, confirmed, high confidence: Classical baseline is misdescribed.
Location: main abstract and lines121-123; supplement:116-123 and decoding table caption. Evidence: src/opm_bridge/models/baselines.py:82-88 defines SIX bands, 1-4, 4-8, 8-13, 13-30, 30-60, and 60-96 Hz. Lines124-129 append FOUR temporal-window means, giving TEN features per channel. scripts/run_nottingham_full_benchmark.py:294 uses this extractor on samples64:268; :304 fits the shared standardized logistic probe whose C=.01 is set in src/opm_bridge/eval/metrics.py:103. Thus the baseline is neither four-band nor purely spectral. This affects the proposed explanation that a simple spectral decoder specifically exposes the missing information. Remedy: report the actual six-band-plus-four-temporal-feature baseline, its input window, shared C=.01 and train-only scaling; remove spectral-only interpretation. Closure: all main/supplement descriptions agree with the extractor and actual classifier call. A pure spectral interpretation would require an ablation, but it is not required for a narrower benchmark claim.

M2. Major, confirmed, high confidence: Decoding evidence does not establish representation failure or absence of a dataset noise floor.
Location: main:20,119-123; supplement:116-126. The eight configurations share ten participants and only four distinct classical results. They cannot serve as eight independent confirmations. Recomputed from saved participant records, two-sided exact sign-flip p values for classical accuracy minus .5 are raw full array .08789, HFC full array .04102, radial .10742, tangential .24023. Participant bootstrap 95% intervals for those differences are respectively [-.000694,.067500], [.010278,.076528], [-.000556,.070139], [-.014583,.067503], using20,000 resamples and seed0. The HFC result is exploratory and marginal before accounting for multiple configurations. Classical-minus-probe exact p values range .03125 to .29883. Observed means consistently favor the baseline, but chance-level means do not prove equivalence, and failure of this fixed probe does not identify information absence in the representation. Remedy: state that reconstruction gains were not accompanied by demonstrated decoding gains in this evaluation; report the four above-chance comparisons and correlated design; replace claims of diagnosed representation failure/noise-floor exclusion with cautious interpretation. Closure: abstract, results and conclusion distinguish observed means, inferential evidence and interpretation. If stronger claims are retained, add a justified label-permutation evaluation of the entire fitting procedure and appropriate multiplicity plan.

M3. Major, confirmed, high confidence: Algebraic percentages are overinterpreted as causal capacity/orientation decomposition.
Location: main:20,88-92,135-140; supplement:98-109. Completing the grid resolves filtering confounding, but random versus monolithic changes both pass count and channel organization. Axis versus random is conditional on ONE random partition and approximately, not exactly, matched group sizes. No monotonicity assumption establishes that its remainder is an upper bound on orientation effects. The text admits these limitations yet states that pass count does one fifth and orientation does the rest, measured directly. Remedy: label these as descriptive proportions of the observed axis-versus-monolithic difference relative to the chosen random control. Say the axis advantage remains under matched filtering. Remove the formal upper-bound claim and general causal allocation. Closure: causal percentages and universal prescription are replaced by conditional descriptive language, or supported by additional controls. Repeated exactly matched random partitions are an optional strengthening if the claim is narrowed.

M6. Minor, confirmed, medium confidence: No-interaction language is stronger than the available test.
Location: main:94, 'nuisance term rather than an interaction'. Remedy: say the axis advantage persists in both regimes and filtering costs are similar in magnitude. Closure: do not claim absence of interaction without a defined equivalence margin or appropriate analysis.
